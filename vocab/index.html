EUonAIR seed vocabularies

Version 0.9 · https://w3id.org/euonair/vocab/

Controlled values used by the EUonAIR AI-Ready Course Description Profile. Each value IRI is https://w3id.org/euonair/vocab/<scheme>/<value>. The AI-use values are input to the alliance guidelines, which own their normative content.

Controlled values. Closed schemes (group mode, grading, generation method, review status, AI permission level, AI disclosure, disclosure scope, input of course materials, rule before the gate) accept only their listed values (Annexes A.6 and C of the MS10 report). The JSON Schema and the SHACL shapes check membership, not only the form of the IRI. In profile version 0.9, cognitive level and competence category are also closed: they accept only the six levels of the revised Bloom taxonomy and the three EQF descriptor categories (Annex A.6), and the JSON Schema checks membership (prefixed form euvoc:…). Other schemes for these two elements, such as SOLO or national outcome models, are deferred to a later version. Unit type accepts any value IRI within its scheme (euvoc:unit-type/…), checked by IRI pattern. Extensible schemes (activity type, assessment method, AI purpose) also accept other IRIs. Such custom values should carry a label, and consuming tools should warn when they meet one. Neither requirement is checked by the validators of this release. The remaining schemes (workload, obligation, difficulty, synchronicity, pace, verification expectation, interaction-mode hint, assessment AI class and assessment setting) list seed values. The validators of this release do not check their values, so they are treated like extensible schemes. Ownership and versioning of the schemes are to be decided in the review of the profile (MS10 report, Section 10).

Permission levels euvoc:ai-permission/

ValueDefinition
not-permittedNo AI use for this scope.
restricted-to-listed-purposesAI use only for the purposes in permittedPurpose.
scaffolding-onlyAI may guide, explain, give hints and give feedback, but must not produce solutions or content that is submitted. Equivalent to permitted purposes {explanation, problem-solving-help in hint form, feedback-on-drafts, exam-practice}.
permitted-with-disclosureAny purpose not listed in excludedPurpose is allowed, provided use is disclosed (disclosure other than none).
unrestrictedAny purpose is allowed. Disclosure only as set in disclosureRequirement.
requiredStudents are expected to use AI for the purposes in permittedPurpose, for example where working with AI is itself a learning outcome. Disclosure as set in disclosureRequirement.

Purposes euvoc:ai-purpose/

ValueDefinition
literature-searchFinding and screening sources.
summarisationSummarising or simplifying reading material.
explanationExplaining concepts or topics (AI as tutor).
problem-solving-helpHelp with tasks or problems step by step. Under scaffolding-only: hints only.
exam-practiceGenerating practice questions or quizzes for self-study.
brainstormingGenerating ideas, structuring a discussion or plan.
language-editingGrammar, style and structure of text the student wrote.
translationTranslating text produced by or for the student.
feedback-on-draftsComments on the student's own draft without rewriting it.
code-assistanceExplaining, debugging or completing code where the student remains the author.
data-analysisHelp with analysing data sets.
generating-submitted-textProducing text that is submitted as (part of) the student's work.
generating-submitted-codeProducing code that is submitted as (part of) the student's work.
generating-mediaProducing images, audio or video for submission.
otherAny other purpose, described in note.

Disclosure requirement euvoc:ai-disclosure/

ValueDefinition
noneNo declaration required.
statementShort declaration of whether and for what purpose AI was used (tool + purpose).
inline-markingEach passage produced or edited with AI is marked in the submission and the tool is named, in the form given in note (as in reference rule (a)).
detailed-logDeclaration plus documentation of the interaction (prompts and key outputs, or a process appendix) and of how outputs were used.

Verification expectation euvoc:ai-verification/

ValueDefinition
none-specifiedNo specific expectation beyond general academic standards.
verify-sources-and-factsStudents must check factual claims and references produced with AI against primary sources.
verify-and-be-able-to-explainAs above, and students must be able to explain or defend any AI-assisted part, for example in an oral follow-up.

Interaction-mode hint for AI tools euvoc:ai-interaction/

ValueDefinition
socraticAsks guiding questions and gives hints. It does not give solutions to the task at hand.
guidedExplains concepts and works analogous examples. It does not solve the assessed task itself.
directMay give complete answers and solutions.

Assessment AI class euvoc:assessment-ai-class/

ValueDefinition
ai-freeThe assessment is designed to be taken without AI support, and no AI use is authorised (typically supervised).
ai-restrictedLimited, specified AI use.
ai-assistedAI use is permitted or required for substantial parts of the task (with disclosure as set).

Disclosure scope euvoc:ai-disclosure-scope/

ValueDefinition
all-useEvery use of AI in the scope is declared, including assistive use such as language editing, translation of the student's own text or reference formatting.
substantial-useOnly use that meaningfully shapes the content, analysis or results of the work is declared. Assistive use (spelling, grammar and style of the student's own text, translation of own text, reference formatting) needs no declaration, provided it does not change the meaning. Verification expectations (C.6) still apply.

Input of course materials euvoc:ai-material-input/

ValueDefinition
not-permittedCourse materials that are not public must not be entered into AI tools.
institutional-tools-onlyCourse materials may be entered only into AI tools that the institution hosts or governs by contract, for example the tools listed in approvedTool.
permittedCourse materials released to students may be entered into any AI tool, within the usage terms of the materials (A13).

Rule before the gate euvoc:before-gate/

ValueDefinition
no-ai-useNo AI use in the scope of the gated policy until the gate is met. This is also the rule when beforeGate is absent.
course-defaultUntil the gate is met, the rule applies that would apply to this scope without the gated policy (precedence rule 1), usually the course rule. Used only in AI conditions of assessment components (A12), at specification or offering level. It is not available on course-level policies (A11), including one given on an offering.
