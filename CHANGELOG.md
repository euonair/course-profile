# Changelog

## 0.9.4 — 31 October 2026

Decisions on the review of report v20. Full example: the AI condition of the team mini-projects states that the team selects and justifies the metrics, interprets the results and makes the release decision itself (note and new assessment criteria). JSON Schema and SHACL shapes require `schemaVersion` on the course unit (R02 is mandatory). Element list: curriculum positions outside a semester calendar are stated in the content description (X30, D07), reviewed and approved translations may be approved language versions (A.0, R14), approved accommodations remain under the institution's authority (precedence rule 7). Value lists and context are unchanged.

## 0.9.3 — 31 October 2026

Corrections after the review of report v20. Workbook: expectation codes E1–E4 in the candidate fields (were A1–A4) and a note on the inferred E3 link of F53. Descriptive SHACL shapes (severity Warning or Info) for properties that Annex A names but the shapes lacked: `copyrightHolder`, `copyrightNotice`, `dcterms:dateCopyrighted` (A13), `euonair:synchronicity`, `euonair:pace` (X32), `courseMode` and `euonair:synchronicity` on the offering (O01). Sub-properties of programme placement (X30) and recognition decisions (X19) are listed as `euonair:` terms. Full example: accessibility summary as a language-tagged string. Website: status of each value scheme on the vocabulary page, citation, `CITATION.cff`, and `profile/0.9/` described as following the current 0.9.x release (each release is a git tag). Value lists, context and JSON Schema are unchanged.

## 0.9.2 — 31 October 2026

Wording of the element definitions, conventions and AI-use rules on the website and in the element list aligned with report v20 (cognitive level and competence category stated as closed lists, expectation codes E1–E4 in the element evidence, clearer precedence rules, revised definition of `ai-free` on the vocabulary page). Value lists, context, schema, shapes and examples are unchanged.

## 0.9.1 — 30 October 2026

Full example record only: the AI condition of the team mini-projects now sets a guided interaction hint and names the outcomes its exclusion of AI-written submitted text protects (LO4, LO5). Vocabulary, context, schema and shapes are unchanged. Profile IRIs stay at version 0.9.

## 0.9 — 2026

First public release, published with EUonAIR Milestone MS10 *AI-Ready Course Descriptions*: 78 elements in four tiers, JSON-LD context, JSON Schema for the core, SHACL shapes, two example records and seed vocabularies.
